ChangeAIPay

AI-native payments with zero transaction fees for local and cross-border payments.

ChangeAIPay is an AI-powered payments application built around two dedicated experiences:

- Personal — for individuals
- Merchant — for businesses

The application combines payment functionality, AI assistance, QR-based payments, Nano, and subscription access into one financial experience.

## What is ChangeAIPay?

ChangeAIPay is designed to simplify how people and businesses send, receive, request, and accept payments.

The product focuses on:

- Zero transaction fees
- Local payments
- Cross-border payments
- Personal and Merchant experiences
- AI-assisted payment interactions
- QR-based payments
- Nano-based payment infrastructure
- Subscription-based platform monetization

## Zero-Transaction-Fee Payments

ChangeAIPay is designed around zero transaction fees for payments.

Supported product flows include:

- Personal → Personal
- Personal → Merchant
- Merchant → Personal
- ChangeAIPay → ChangeAIPay
- Local payments
- Cross-border payments
- ChangeAIPay → supported external payment apps or wallets

Currency conversion and foreign-exchange costs are separate from transaction fees and may apply where applicable.

## Personal

The Personal experience is designed for individuals, including students, creators, freelancers, and everyday users.

### Features

- Fina AI Assistant
- Send and receive payments
- Request money
- QR payment interactions
- Nano wallet experience
- Cross-border payment experience
- Payment history
- Notifications
- Currency and FX information
- Personal subscription access

## Merchant

The Merchant experience is designed for merchants, small businesses, creators, and other businesses that accept payments.

### Features

- Merchant dashboard
- Accept payments
- Merchant QR payment flow
- Personal → Merchant payment experience
- Payment activity
- Merchant subscription access

## Fina AI

Fina is the Personal AI assistant inside ChangeAIPay.

It is designed to provide a conversational interface for interacting with payment functionality and help simplify common payment actions.

The application also retains conventional user-interface flows so payment functionality does not depend exclusively on conversational interaction.

## Nano

ChangeAIPay is designed around the Nano network for its payment infrastructure.

The Nano-based payment experience is intended to support fast digital payments without a transaction fee on the payment itself.

## QR Payments

ChangeAIPay includes QR-based payment experiences for both Personal and Merchant users.

Merchants can use a dedicated payment flow, while Personal users can interact with merchant payment requests through the corresponding payment experience.

## Cross-Border Payments

ChangeAIPay is designed to support cross-border payments without charging a transaction fee.

The product separates transaction fees from any applicable currency conversion or foreign-exchange costs.

## External Transfers

ChangeAIPay is designed to support transfers from ChangeAIPay to supported external payment applications or wallets without a transaction fee.

Availability depends on the supported payment route and integration.

## Subscriptions

ChangeAIPay uses RevenueCat for subscription and entitlement management.

Separate subscription experiences are provided for Personal and Merchant users.

The subscription model is designed to monetize the platform without introducing a transaction fee for payments.

## Project Status

This repository contains the ChangeAIPay application and its source code.

The current project demonstrates the Personal and Merchant experiences and the corresponding product flows.

Payment interactions shown in the application are demo/prototype flows and should not be interpreted as real-money transactions.

The submitted project does not claim to have real users, real transaction history, or production payment activity.

The zero-transaction-fee model described above represents the intended ChangeAIPay product model.
